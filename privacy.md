# Privacy Policy – LookCraft

Last updated: October 2026

LookCraft is a wardrobe app. It keeps an inventory of your clothes and suggests outfits for a piece you choose.

**Information you add.** Photos and details of your clothing items, such as category, color, brand and notes. You decide what to add.

**How it is used.** Your wardrobe information is used only to run the app: showing your closet and generating outfit suggestions. To generate suggestions, details of the selected item and your other items may be sent to a third-party AI service. That data is used only to produce the suggestion.

**Pinterest (optional).** If you connect your Pinterest account, the app reads your own boards and pins, read-only, to show an inspiration image next to a suggestion. It does not post to Pinterest and does not copy or store Pinterest pins or data. It keeps an access token so you stay signed in. If you disconnect Pinterest, the app stops reading from it and removes the token.

**What we do not do.** No selling of your data. Your data is not shared with anyone except the services described above.

**Deleting your data.** You can delete your items at any time. To request deletion of all your data, open an issue on this repository.

**Not affiliated with Pinterest.** LookCraft is independent and is not endorsed by or affiliated with Pinterest.

**Contact:** open an issue at https://github.com/minenotavailable/lookcraft/issues
